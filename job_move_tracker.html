Read this before acting on any row

  • Detected date is not move date. Apollo records when it spotted the change. The person may have moved weeks earlier.
  • Some matches are wrong. At least one sponsor appears with an unrelated new role. Check the LinkedIn URL in the spreadsheet before any outreach.
  • Deal values are not ACV. HubSpot amounts on these accounts look like monthly or per-contract figures. Treat them as relative scale only.
  • 451 of 1,202 movers were excluded because they have no HubSpot link — Apollo-only prospects, not platform users.
  • Three overlapping role properties exist in HubSpot (platform_user_access_role, platform_account_type, platform_role) and they disagree. This uses the union. Worth consolidating.
